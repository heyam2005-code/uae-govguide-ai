# Candidate global design comparators (not UAE legal requirements)

1. **EU GDPR Art. 5 (data minimisation and accuracy)**: EU data-protection law requires personal data to be limited to what is necessary and kept accurate. As a *design comparator*, ask applicants for status (citizen/resident), expiry windows and availability of photo, not identity numbers. Source: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng/
2. **UK GDS API standards**: UK government API guidance favours API-first design, interoperable standards, and explicit GeoJSON where geographic data is exchanged. As a *design comparator*, propose an authorized, documented service-centre dataset with stable IDs, current status, lat/lon and service scope. Source: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/gds-api-technical-and-data-standards

## Gap questions to validate with UAE stakeholders

- Is a versioned, machine-readable catalogue of required documents by applicant category and emirate available for authorized agents?
- Which entity should publish authoritative machine-readable cross-emirate process dependencies?
- Are live centre-specific service availability, accessibility, actual working hours and queue/appointment slots published through an authorized interface?

These are **questions and opportunities**, not claims that a UAE regulator or agency is noncompliant. Do not infer a statutory conflict from limitations of this prototype.
